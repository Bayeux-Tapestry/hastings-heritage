Aaron Goree

Competition winner

I was born in 1974 with deep family roots bound to Hastings. I create under the artist pseudonym 'The Angry George'. My art is an expression of the vibrant energy, coastal character and rich tapestry of local legend and history that define my home town. Visually, my style merges bold, graphic line with geometric stained glass - like pattern using a high contrast colour palette. I enjoy capturing iconic landmarks such as Hastings Pier, The Net Huts, and Bottle Alley. My artwork is heavily driven by my fascination with local folklore, historical milestones like 1066 and the raw energy of music and punk culture and art scene. My paintings celebrate the unique spirit and community of Hastings. As an amateur artist, every piece I create reflects a personal 'visual diary'- a collision of historic pride, music culture and contemporary coastal life mapped out in bold, defiant colour.
